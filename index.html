Youth Empowerment Programme

Our Golf for Change Youth Empowerment Programme aims to introduce and support young people aged 6–21 through golf, mentorship and life-skills development.

Through access to golf and positive guidance, the programme seeks to help young people build confidence, discipline, teamwork and a sense of possibility—on and off the course.

Help us open more opportunities for young people.

Golf for Change Charity Tournament

Our charity golf tournament will bring golfers, businesses and supporters together for a day of sport, networking and giving back.

The tournament is intended to raise support for the youth empowerment programme and help more young people access golf, mentorship and development opportunities.

Interested in playing, sponsoring or contributing prizes? Get in touch to register your interest.

Connect With Sponsors

We welcome individuals, companies, golf clubs and community partners who would like to support Golf for Change.

Support can include financial contributions, golf clubs and other used golf equipment in good condition, tournament prizes, training opportunities, mentorship or in-kind services.

Every contribution can help us create more opportunities for young people and strengthen the charity tournament.
